Lesson 2: Basic Greetings

Master essential greeting phrases and polite expressions used in everyday Telugu conversations. These are the foundation for any meaningful interaction.

Common Greetings

Learn how to greet people in different situations. Telugu greetings vary based on time of day and level of formality.

Morning, Afternoon & Evening Greetings

Telugu Romanized Form English Meaning When to Use
నమస్కారం Namaskaaram Hello / Good day Anytime, formal greeting
ఉదయస్సాగరం Udayassaagaram Good morning Early morning (6-11 AM)
సుస్వాగతం Susvaagastam Welcome When welcoming someone
రాత్రి బాగుందా? Rathri baguandaa? Good evening? Evening time (after 6 PM)
శుభరాత్రి Shubha rathri Good night Before sleeping

Formal Greetings

Telugu Romanized Form English Meaning
ఎలా ఉన్నారు? Ela unnaru? How are you? (formal)
ఎలా సులుకుంటున్నారు? Ela sulukuntunaaru? How is everything going? (formal)
మీరు ఎలా ఉన్నారు? Meeru ela unnaru? How are you? (very formal/respectful)

Informal Greetings

Telugu Romanized Form English Meaning
ఎలా ఉన్నావ? Ela unnava? How are you? (informal)
బాగుందా? Baguundaa? How are you doing? (casual)
చాలా బాగొ Chala bago Very good / Excellent

Responses to Greetings

Learn how to respond appropriately to greetings and questions about your well-being.

Telugu Romanized Form English Meaning
బాగున్నాను Bagunnanu I am fine (informal)
చాలా బాగున్నాను Chala bagunnanu I am very fine (informal)
ధన్యవాదాలు Dhanyavaadalu Thank you
ఆ విధంగా, మీరు ఎలా ఉన్నారు? Aa vidhanga, meeru ela unnaru? I'm like that, how are you? (formal return greeting)
కూడా బాగున్నాను Kooda bagunnanu I'm also fine (informal)

Polite Expressions

Essential polite phrases for respectful communication.

Telugu Romanized Form English Meaning
దయచేసి Dayacheesi Please
ధన్యవాదాలు Dhanyavaadalu Thank you
చాలా ధన్యవాదాలు Chala dhanyavaadalu Thank you very much
క్షమించండి Kshaminchandi Excuse me / I'm sorry (formal)
సార్రీ Sorry Sorry (informal)
కూల గమనం Kuul gamnam No problem / It's okay

Dialogue Practice

Here's a typical greeting conversation to practice:

Person A: నమస్కారం!
Namaskaaram!
Hello!
Person B: నమస్కారం! ఎలా ఉన్నారు?
Namaskaaram! Ela unnaru?
Hello! How are you?
Person A: చాలా బాగున్నాను! ధన్యవాదాలు. మీరు?
Chala bagunnanu! Dhanyavaadalu. Meeru?
I'm very fine, thank you. And you?
Person B: నేను కూడా బాగున్నాను!
Nenu kooda bagunnanu!
I'm also fine!

✏️ Practice Exercises

Exercise 1: Greeting Memorization

Memorize at least 5 basic greetings and their responses. Write them down 3 times each.

Exercise 2: Time-Based Greetings

Create a schedule: Use "ఉదయస్సాగరం" (udayassaagaram) in the morning, "నమస్కారం" (namaskaaram) in the afternoon, and "శుభరాత్రి" (shubha rathri) in the evening.

Exercise 3: Dialogue Practice

Practice the dialogue above with a partner or speak it out loud. Focus on correct pronunciation and natural flow.

Exercise 4: Response Training

Have someone ask you "ఎలా ఉన్నారు?" (ela unnaru?) and practice responding naturally with different variations.

Exercise 5: Cultural Context

Learn when to use formal vs. informal greetings. Formal greetings are typically used with elders, teachers, and strangers.

Cultural Notes

  • Respect Through Language: Telugu has different levels of formality. Using formal greetings (like "మీరు ఎలా ఉన్నారు?") shows respect.
  • Traditional vs. Modern: "నమస్కారం" is traditional and widely used across all age groups. "హాయ్" (Hi) is becoming popular among younger generations.
  • Gender Neutral: Most greetings in Telugu are gender-neutral, which is convenient for learners.
  • Physical Greetings: In formal settings, a simple nod or folded hands (namaste gesture) accompanies verbal greetings.
