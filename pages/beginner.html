Tips for Successful Learning

📅 Practice Daily

Spend at least 15 minutes daily. Consistency is more important than duration.

🎧 Pronounce Out Loud

Always speak the words aloud to develop proper pronunciation and muscle memory.

✏️ Write & Repeat

Write Telugu characters and words multiple times to improve writing skills.

🔄 Review Regularly

Go back to previous lessons weekly to ensure you remember what you learned.
